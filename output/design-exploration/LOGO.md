# Logo direction

Brand: devegoma’s blog

The exploration uses a compact editorial wordmark treatment: deep purple lettering (#322257), a small vivid-purple detail (#5730bd), and a transparent field. It should be displayed as a linked brand mark in the page header with alt text `devegoma’s blog`, at a height of about 36 px and with its aspect ratio preserved. Generate a brand-specific transparent WebP from this direction before carrying the design into the production site; this exploration uses a text mark so all three compositions remain self-contained.

The three proposals share the blog’s actual subject matter and avoid invented readership, publishing frequency, or impact claims. Their headline and program contents are illustrative editorial copy based on the agreed blog scope.
